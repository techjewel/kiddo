## Bangla letters
The বর্ণমালা in `sounds/bangla/letters/` is the same kind of thing as the
numbers: Lingua Libre recordings from Wikimedia Commons, real speakers,
one letter or one word each, CC0. Cut the same way — trimmed to the
sound, a breath of silence at each end, levelled to the loudness of
every other clip on the board.

Each letter is two files: `<n>.m4a` is the letter and `<n>-word.m4a` is
the word it starts, played straight after it. Eight letters — ঙ, ঞ, ণ, য,
ড়, ঢ়, য় and ৎ — start no word a small child meets, and have only the
first file.

Eighty-four of the eighty-six clips are read by **Titodutta**, the same
voice as the numbers and the body parts. Lingua Libre has no ঊষা or
খরগোশ from him, so those two words are read by **Tahmid** — the smallest
seam available, and it falls on the word rather than on a letter.

## Bangla numbers
The twenty number words in `sounds/bangla/` are Lingua Libre recordings
from Wikimedia Commons — real speakers, one word each, released into the
public domain under CC0. Each was trimmed to the word, given a short lead-in
and tail of silence, and levelled to the same loudness as the animal clips.
No other changes were made.

Fifteen are read by **Titodutta**. Lingua Libre has no তেরো, পনেরো, সতেরো,
আঠারো or উনিশ from that speaker, so ১৩, ১৫, ১৭, ১৮ and ১৯ are read by
**Galpadattya** — the smallest seam available, since no single speaker has
recorded all twenty.

## Bangla body parts
The eighteen body-part words in `sounds/bangla/body/` are Lingua Libre
recordings from Wikimedia Commons, cut the same way as the numbers above:
trimmed to the word, a breath of silence at each end, levelled to the same
loudness. All CC0 (public domain).

Sixteen are read by **Titodutta**, the same speaker as most of the numbers.
Lingua Libre has no কনুই from him, so *elbows* is read by **Symoum Syfullah
Priyo**. Bengali has no one-word name for the flat of the foot or for a toe,
so *feet* and *toes* are two of his recordings joined — পায়ের then পাতা, and
পায়ের then আঙুল — the way the pig above is three oinks joined.
